About the Doctor

Care That Helps You Get Back to Life.

Dr. Hemant Jain is an orthopaedic surgeon based in Jodhpur, Rajasthan, with a clinical focus reflected in arthroscopy, sports injuries and joint-related orthopaedic conditions.

His areas of practice include knee ligament injuries, meniscus problems, shoulder instability and other orthopaedic conditions requiring careful evaluation and treatment.

The goal of orthopaedic care is not just treating an injury, but helping patients work towards restoring mobility, function and confidence in everyday life.
